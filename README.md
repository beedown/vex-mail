# VEX Mail

**Every inbox. One place.** A dark, fast email app with a smart calendar, made by beedown.

## What VEX does

- **All your email in one inbox.** Connect Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud and most other email services, with as many addresses as you like. Name and color each one, read them together or one at a time, and pick which address to send from.
- **Sorts itself.** Every email gets tagged Needs reply, Waiting on, FYI or Junk, and newsletters fold into one row. VEX learns which senders matter to you, and you can tell it "always junk" or "always important" for any sender.
- **Smart help.** A Today card shows who's waiting on you, what's due and what's next on your calendar. Login codes get a copy button. Quick replies fit the email, in English or Dutch. Dates in emails turn into due dates, reminders and calendar events.
- **Sent mail.** See everything you sent from all your addresses, with earlier emails shown under each conversation.
- **Search that predicts what you type.** It suggests people, words and filters as you type, fixes typos, and learns what you pick.
- **Smart calendar.** Paste your school timetable, in English or Dutch, or add a calendar link from Google Calendar, Smartschool or Outlook. Then tell VEX what you want to do, like gaming 5 times a week or 4 hours of study before a test, and it plans that into your free time.
- **Email the way it should be.** Emails show as designed, with tracking pixels blocked and pictures only when you want them. Open and send attachments, unsubscribe with one tap, browse your folders and get deleted email back, and search all your old mail on the server.
- **Alerts and reminders.** New-email alerts with quiet hours, reminders before events and planned sessions, and a home screen widget on Android.
- **Focus, stats and achievements.** During study time VEX holds back email that isn't important. See your week, keep streaks going and unlock achievements.
- **Phone and PC in sync.** Your calendar, plans and settings sync through a hidden folder in your own mailbox, with no extra servers.
- **Quality of life.** Swipe to archive or snooze, undo send, send later, signatures, saved replies, reminders when nobody replies, saved drafts, an app lock with PIN or fingerprint, and keyboard shortcuts on PC.
- **Yours to style.** Six accent colors, three title fonts, sharp or soft corners, five original etched art scenes, and moving fog, rain or embers.
- **Private.** No ads, no trackers and no VEX servers. Your email goes straight between your device and your email provider.

## Download

All downloads are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/beedown/vex-mail/releases).

### Android phone (Android 8 or newer)

1. On your phone, open the newest **VEX for Android** release and tap **VEX.apk**.
2. If Chrome warns you about the file, tap **Download anyway**, then **Open**.
3. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, and go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.

Android shows these warnings for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

### Windows PC

1. Open the newest **VEX for PC** release and download **VEX-Setup.exe**.
2. Open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Windows shows this for apps that don't have a paid signing certificate.
3. VEX installs, opens, and adds itself to your Start menu and desktop.

### iPhone

There's no iPhone version of VEX yet.

## Connect your email

1. In VEX, open **Settings** and tap **Connect**.
2. Pick your email provider, then enter your address and an **app password**. An app password is a separate password just for VEX, so you never type in your real one.
   - **Gmail:** turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google account, then create an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
   - **Yahoo:** create an app password in your Yahoo account's security settings.
   - **iCloud:** create an app-specific password in your Apple account.
   - **Others:** choose **Other** and enter your provider's incoming (IMAP) and outgoing (SMTP) servers.

**Hotmail and Outlook:** choose **Hotmail** in Connect and tap **Sign in with Microsoft**. You sign in on Microsoft's own page, and VEX never sees your password. If that button isn't there, this copy of VEX hasn't been set up for Microsoft sign-in yet; the same tab shows how to forward Hotmail to Gmail instead.

## Smart calendar

1. Open the **Calendar** tab and tap **Import** to paste your timetable or calendar text, or a calendar link (the iCal link from Google Calendar, Smartschool or Outlook).
2. Type what you want to do in the box at the top, in your own words, in English or Dutch. VEX's built-in assistant works it out and shows its plan before you add it:
   - "gaming 2h tonight" or "dentist next week": VEX picks a time that makes sense (dentists on weekdays, gaming in the evening after homework, Chiro on Sunday afternoon), says why, and offers other times.
   - "study for math test friday": adds the test and plans study sessions on the days before.
   - "gaming 3 times this week" or "piano every other monday at 17u until christmas": spreads sessions out or repeats them.
   - "gaming after voetbal wednesday": plans around what's already in your calendar.
   - "gaming tonight and voetbal tomorrow at 17u": several things at once.
   - Typos like "tomorow" or "wensday" are fine, and VEX shows how it read them.
   - Buttons let you make it shorter, longer, earlier, later or move it to another day.
3. Ask it things: "when am I free friday?", "what's on tomorrow?", "when is my next math test?", "how busy is my week?" or "when can I game this week?"
4. VEX learns from you. When you finish sessions or pick times, it notices when you like to do things, including your own activities, and plans them around then.

The assistant runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

## Updates

VEX checks for new versions by itself when you open it and installs them. On Android, tap **Update** if Android asks. You can turn this off in **Settings**, under **App updates**.

## Privacy

VEX saves your email passwords, emails and calendar only on your device. It talks to your email providers, calendar links you add, GitHub (for updates) and Google Fonts, and nothing else. Read the full [privacy policy and terms of use](PRIVACY.md).
