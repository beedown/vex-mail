# VEX privacy policy and terms of use

Last updated November 9, 2026

## Privacy policy

VEX is made by beedown. In short: VEX has no servers and no ads, it doesn't track you, and beedown never sees your email, passwords or calendar. Everything stays on your device.

### Your email accounts

When you connect an email address, you give VEX the address and an app password. They're saved only on your device: on Android in VEX's private app storage, which isn't included in phone backups, and on Windows encrypted with Windows' own protection. VEX uses them only to connect straight to your email provider, like Gmail or Yahoo, over an encrypted connection. Remove an account in Settings and its password is deleted.

### Your emails

VEX downloads your newest emails straight from your provider and keeps a copy on your device so it can show them, sort them and search them. Reading, starring, archiving, deleting and sending go straight to your provider. Your emails are never sent anywhere else.

### Your calendar and plans

Your calendar, plans and settings are saved on your device. If you add a calendar link, VEX downloads that calendar straight from the link's address.

### Who VEX talks to

- Your email providers, to get and send your mail.
- Calendar links you add.
- GitHub, to check for and download VEX updates.
- Google Fonts, to load VEX's fonts.

Like any website, these services can see your device's internet address when VEX connects. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

### What VEX doesn't do

No VEX account, no ads, no analytics or trackers, and no selling or sharing of your data. VEX's smart features, like sorting, predictions and planning, all run on your device.

### The version inside Claude

If you use VEX through its Claude link and sign in with your Claude account, your VEX settings and sample data sync through that account, and Anthropic's privacy policy applies to it.

### Deleting your data

Remove email accounts in Settings, and remove calendars under Calendar, then Plan. Uninstalling VEX deletes everything it saved on your device.

### Questions

Open an issue on [VEX's GitHub page](https://github.com/beedown/vex-mail/issues). If this policy changes, the new version comes with a VEX update and the date above changes.
